Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

⚠️ Important: Video Maker App operates entirely on your local device. We do not operate cloud servers or collect personal data beyond what is necessary for the App to function.

1. Information We Collect

Video Maker App collects and processes the following types of information:

1.1 Authentication Credentials

  • TikTok Access Token: OAuth token to access TikTok Content Posting API
  • YouTube Refresh Token: OAuth token to upload videos to YouTube
  • Facebook Access Token: Page access token for Facebook Reels
  • API Keys: For ElevenLabs, AssemblyAI, and Pexels services

Storage: All credentials are stored locally in your .env file. They never leave your device except when making authorized API calls.

1.2 Video Content

  • Video files you create are temporarily stored during processing
  • Text content used for video generation
  • Generated audio and subtitle files

Storage: All files are stored locally in the output/ and temp/ directories. Temporary files are automatically deleted after upload.

1.3 Usage Data

  • Basic console logs of upload operations
  • Error messages for troubleshooting

Storage: Logs are displayed in your terminal only. No persistent logs are stored.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Upload Videos: To post videos to your authorized TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook accounts
  • Generate Metadata: To create titles, descriptions, and hashtags for your videos using AI
  • Process Media: To generate voiceovers, subtitles, and combine video elements
  • Troubleshoot Issues: To display error messages when problems occur

3. Data Storage and Security

Data Type Storage Location Retention
API Tokens Local .env file Until manually deleted
Final Videos Local output/ folder Until manually deleted
Temporary Files Local temp/ folder Auto-deleted after upload
Console Logs Terminal output only Not persisted
🔒 Security Measures:
  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • No cloud storage or remote servers are used
  • API tokens are stored in environment files (should not be committed to Git)
  • HTTPS is used for all API communications

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services. Your data may be transmitted to these services:

4.1 Social Media Platforms

  • TikTok: Videos and metadata are uploaded. See TikTok Privacy Policy
  • YouTube: Videos and metadata are uploaded. See Google Privacy Policy
  • Facebook: Videos and metadata are uploaded. See Meta Privacy Policy

4.2 Media Processing Services

  • ElevenLabs: Text is sent for voice generation. See ElevenLabs Privacy Policy
  • AssemblyAI: Audio files are sent for transcription. See AssemblyAI Privacy Policy
  • Pexels: API requests for background videos. See Pexels Privacy Policy
  • Ollama: Text generation happens locally (if self-hosted). See Ollama Privacy

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties.

Data is only transmitted to:

  • The social media platforms you authorize (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook)
  • Media processing services necessary for App functionality

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data:

6.1 Access and Control

  • View Data: All data is stored locally in the App directory
  • Export Data: Copy files from output/ folder at any time
  • Delete Data: Delete the App folder to remove all local data

6.2 Revoke Access

You can revoke the App's access to your accounts at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings → Privacy → Apps → Remove Video Maker App
  • YouTube: Google Account Permissions
  • Facebook: Settings → Business Integrations → Remove App

6.3 Data Deletion

To permanently delete all App data:

  1. Delete the App directory from your computer
  2. Revoke API access through platform settings (above)
  3. Delete videos from social media platforms manually

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

The App processes data locally on your device. However, when you upload videos to social media platforms, your data may be transferred internationally according to each platform's data practices.

9. Data Retention

  • API Tokens: Retained until you manually delete them from .env
  • Videos: Retained in output/ until you manually delete them
  • Temporary Files: Automatically deleted after each upload
  • Uploaded Content: Retained by social media platforms per their policies

10. Security Practices

We recommend the following security practices:

  • Never commit your .env file to version control (Git)
  • Keep your API tokens confidential
  • Regularly rotate your access tokens
  • Use strong passwords for your platform accounts
  • Keep your system and dependencies updated

11. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools. No behavioral data is collected.

12. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Legal Compliance

We comply with applicable data protection laws including:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) for EU users
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) for California users
  • Other applicable local data protection regulations

14. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request copies of your data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how data is used
  • Right to Data Portability: Export your data
  • Right to Object: Object to data processing

Since all data is stored locally, you can exercise these rights directly by managing files on your device.
